Clarifying Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our heirloom recipes, non-veg preparation, storage, and shipping.

How long do your pickles last once opened?

Our classic vegetarian pickles stay fresh for 12 months, while meat pickles (Andhra Chicken, Mutton, Prawns) stay peak delicious for 6 months. Always use a dry spoon and ensure a thin layer of oil rests over the top to naturally preserve freshness without artificial chemicals.

How are the Non-Veg pickles prepared safely?

Our non-veg pickles are prepared under strict hygienic conditions. Fresh boneless chicken chunks, coastal prawns, and tender goat meat are slow-fried in wood-churned cold-pressed groundnut oil until crisp and moisture-free, then steeped with ground spices and lemon juice for an irresistible umami punch.

Do you use plastic bottles or artificial preservatives?

Never! We strictly use heavy food-grade glass jars and airtight lids. We never add synthetic sodium benzoate, artificial colors, or chemical preservatives. Natural rock salt, turmeric, lemon juice, and cold-pressed oils are our only preservatives.

How fast is shipping and is it free?

Every batch dispatches within 24–48 hours of order confirmation. Delivery takes 3–5 business days across all Indian PIN codes. Orders over ₹500 enjoy 100% FREE delivery nationwide.

How does WhatsApp direct order and UPI payment work?

When you click checkout, your selected items and address format into a clean WhatsApp message sent straight to our artisan kitchen line. You can scan our direct UPI QR code or pay via GPay/PhonePe/Paytm with zero payment gateway processing fees.
